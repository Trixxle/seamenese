Jai-Semen (Verb Conjugation Dojo)

In Seamenese, Ëi (I) and Te (You sg.) use the bare stem, Ton (You pl.) adds -u, Na (We) adds -ami, and La / Lon (He/She/It/They) add -ai.

Zorpe-Semen (The Interrogative Triplet)

• Wa (What): Triggers object fronting or copula questions ("Wa te zete?" = What do you eat?, "Wa xaai et?" = What is this?).
• We (Why): Keeps standard Subject-Verb-Object order after We ("We te xa sarpo?" = Why are you tired?).
• Wu (How): Used in copula state questions ("Wu xa te?" = How are you?, "Wu xaai etpanke?" = How is today?).
