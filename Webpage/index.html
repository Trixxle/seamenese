NEW • Official Plural Rule (-on)

Formula: <noun> minus last vowel + on
• 1 sobo (1 foot) → 2 sobon (2 feet)
• 1 buana (1 friend) → 2 buanon (2 friends)
• 1 skantar (1 crush/lover) → 2 skantaron (2 crushes/lovers)
• 1 texik (1 asshole) → 2 texikon (2 assholes)
• 1 zorpe (1 question) → 2 zorpon (2 questions)

Pronunciation & Multi-Verb Chaining Rules

• Vowel Ë (Diaeresis): E followed by I always makes Ë (e.g., Ëi, Zetëi, Obisëi, Mikëi, Zorpëi). This is because Ëi is pronounced "ay", whereas normally E is always pronounced like "a" in "amp".
• Consonant X: Always pronounced as "sh" (e.g., Xai = "shai", Texik = "teshik", Brux = "brush").
• Multi-Verb Rule: When using more than one verb that refers to a single pronoun or subject, only the first verb is conjugated; the second stays in the infinitive! E.g.: "Ëi skan obisëi ezuki" (I like to sing), "Ëi wo skan itxi tëi texik".
• Singing & Amplifier Wo: There is no single verb for "singing" — Seamenese uses "Obisëi ezuki" (vocalize music). And Wo is both "big" and an amplifier: Ëi skan = I like, Ëi wo skan = I love!
